How long does it take?+

Usually three weeks from kick-off. Very small businesses often need less time, larger ones a little more. We agree the timing upfront.

How much of our time does it need?+

A few hours in total: a kick-off with you, short interviews with the people who do the work, and the final presentation.

Do you build or implement the AI?+

Yes. In week three we set up your first quick win with you and train your team to use it. After the audit, we can build the rest of your roadmap. For every step, we either build it or recommend the best existing solution, whichever gets the same result for less. If we recommend a tool, you buy it directly from the provider.

What if you find nothing worth doing?+

Then you get a clear "not yet", with the reasons and what would need to change. That alone can save you from buying tools that don't fit.

What happens to our data?+

We treat it as confidential, can work under NDA, and only use what the audit needs. Data protection is part of every recommendation.
